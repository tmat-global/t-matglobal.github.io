Common Questions

Offshore Team FAQ

How does T-Mat Global build offshore engineering teams?
T-Mat Global builds offshore teams through a structured process: role definition, candidate vetting by the founding team, a trial engagement on a scoped deliverable, and then ongoing team operation with weekly standups, sprint reviews, and monthly reporting. Teams operate with async-first workflows and clear communication protocols.
What does an offshore engineering team from India cost?
A 3-engineer team (1 senior backend, 1 frontend, 1 DevOps) typically runs $6,000–$10,000 per month — 60–70% less than equivalent US or UK teams. T-Mat Global charges per milestone, not per hour. Teams scale each quarter based on delivery needs.
Who owns the code built by the offshore team?
You do — from day one. All code is committed to your repository. Full IP assignment is included in the contract, alongside NDAs for every team member. DPIIT recognition provides legal standing for cross-border IP transfer.
