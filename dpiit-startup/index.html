Common Questions

DPIIT Recognition FAQ

What is DPIIT recognition for Indian startups?
DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) recognition is a Government of India certification for eligible startups under the Startup India initiative. It confirms that the company meets legal incorporation requirements, innovation criteria, and is not a restructured older entity. Recognition provides legal standing for tax benefits, fast-track patent processing, and simplified regulatory compliance.
What does DPIIT recognition mean for international clients?
For US, UAE and UK clients: (1) the company is legally verified by the Indian government, (2) it meets innovation and compliance criteria, (3) it is not a shell or fake entity, (4) contracts have clearer legal recourse. It is one of the strongest credibility signals an Indian startup can provide to international buyers.
