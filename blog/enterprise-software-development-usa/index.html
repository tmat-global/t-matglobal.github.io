Enterprise Software Development for US Companies: India-Based Delivery That Performs

T-Mat Global was founded by a former T-Mobile USA engineer specifically to serve US enterprise clients with India-based delivery. We operate in US time zones, understand American enterprise governance requirements, and structure contracts to the standards US legal and procurement teams expect.

By T-Mat Global Published April 13, 2026 6 min read

T-Mat Global's founding story is inseparable from its US connection. Sainath Mitalakar, the founder, worked as a DevOps engineer on T-Mobile USA's System Design and Architecture team — one of the largest and most demanding infrastructure environments in American telecom. That experience shaped T-Mat Global's engineering standards, its process discipline, and its understanding of what US enterprise clients actually require from a software development partner.

The result is an India-based engineering firm that was built to serve US clients from the ground up — not adapted to serve them after the fact.

Working in US Time Zones

The time zone gap between India and the US — 9.5 to 12.5 hours depending on timezone — is the most common concern US clients raise when evaluating India-based partners. T-Mat Global manages this through explicit overlap commitments. For US East Coast clients, we maintain a 3–4 hour daily overlap window in early morning IST that covers US business hours. For West Coast clients, we provide early morning overlap or asynchronous-first workflows with guaranteed same-business-day response SLAs.

All client-facing communication uses tools US engineering teams already use — Slack, Jira, GitHub, Notion. We do not require clients to adopt new tools to work with us.
