DPIIT Recognized Startup India: What It Means for Software Clients

When evaluating an Indian software company, DPIIT recognition is one of the clearest signals of legitimacy. Here is what it actually verifies, what it does not, and how to use it when vetting a vendor.

By Sainath Mitalakar April 23, 2026 9 min read

What DPIIT Recognition Is

The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) is a ministry under the Government of India responsible for the Startup India initiative. DPIIT recognition is a formal government certification that verifies a company as an eligible startup — one that is incorporated, innovative, and operating within the defined parameters of the Startup India program.

Recognition is not automatic. A company must apply through the Startup India portal, provide incorporation documents, demonstrate that the business model involves innovation or technology, and pass a review by the DPIIT. The certificate issued is tied to a specific CIN (Corporate Identification Number) — the legal identifier that makes the company traceable on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) national database.

What DPIIT Recognition Verifies

Legal Incorporation

The company is incorporated under the Companies Act — a registered private limited company or LLP with a government-assigned CIN.

Government Audit Trail

Annual filings with the MCA are required to maintain registration. A recognized company has a verifiable public record of directors, filings, and history.

Innovation Classification

The DPIIT review process requires a business description that demonstrates technology-led or innovative activity — not a commodity trading or reselling operation.

Tax Standing

DPIIT-recognized startups are eligible for Section 80-IAC income tax exemptions — they must maintain their tax filing status to retain recognition benefits.

What It Does Not Verify

DPIIT recognition is a legitimacy signal, not a quality guarantee. It tells you the company exists legally and is what it claims to be — it does not tell you whether the team is technically capable or has delivered comparable projects. Use it as a floor, not a ceiling, in your evaluation.

DPIIT does not verify
  • Technical capability or seniority of the engineering team
  • Quality of past project deliveries
  • IP assignment practices in contracts
  • Actual company size (a solo founder can be DPIIT recognized)
  • Whether the company is currently active or dormant

Why It Matters for International Clients

The most common concern for a US, UK, or UAE buyer engaging an Indian software company is: "Is this a real company or an individual working under a business name?" DPIIT recognition — combined with a CIN verification on MCA — answers that question definitively.

"A CIN number you can look up on the MCA portal is worth more than a LinkedIn company page with 500 followers and no verifiable history."

How to Verify a DPIIT Certificate

  1. Ask the vendor for their DPIIT certificate (issued by the Startup India portal — a PDF with a unique reference number)
  2. Ask for their CIN (Corporate Identification Number — 21 characters, format U12345MH2024PTC000000)
  3. Visit mca.gov.in → MCA21 portal → Company Search → enter the CIN
  4. Verify: date of incorporation, registered state, company status (Active/Struck Off), director names
  5. Cross-reference director names against LinkedIn profiles of the team you are talking to

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DPIIT recognition for a startup in India?
DPIIT (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade) recognition is a government certification for Indian startups that verifies the entity as an innovative, technology-led business. It requires incorporation proof and review by the Ministry of Commerce. Recognition grants access to tax exemptions, regulatory benefits, and government procurement opportunities, and is tied to a verifiable CIN on the MCA national database.
Why does DPIIT recognition matter when hiring an Indian software company?
DPIIT recognition means the company is a verified legal entity — government-audited, CIN registered, with a documented founding history and annual filings on record. For international clients, it distinguishes a serious registered company from an individual freelancer operating under a business name with no verifiable legal standing.
How do I verify if an Indian software company is DPIIT recognized?
Ask for the DPIIT certificate (issued by Startup India) and the CIN (Corporate Identification Number). The CIN can be cross-referenced on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs portal at mca.gov.in to verify registration status, directors, and filing history. The DPIIT certificate number can also be verified on the Startup India government portal.
What other certifications should I check for an Indian software vendor?
Beyond DPIIT, look for: AWS/Azure/GCP certifications for cloud work (all verifiable online with a certificate ID), ISO 27001 for information security, NASSCOM membership for larger firms, and a live project portfolio with demonstrable systems. For regulated industries, ask about HIPAA awareness or GDPR data handling practices in their contract templates.
