What Custom ERP Software Actually Includes

A custom ERP is a purpose-built enterprise application that manages the core operational workflows of a business within a single integrated system. Unlike off-the-shelf ERP products like SAP or Oracle NetSuite — which are built to serve thousands of different business types and require extensive configuration and consulting to deploy — a custom ERP is designed specifically for one business or one business type, which means it fits the operational model from day one rather than requiring the business to adapt itself to the software.

The modules that comprise a custom ERP vary by industry and business type, but the core categories are consistent across most implementations:

  • Financial management — general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, bank reconciliation, multi-currency, tax management, financial reporting
  • Inventory and supply chain — stock management, purchase orders, supplier management, goods receipt, multi-location inventory, reorder automation
  • Sales and CRM — customer records, quotation management, sales orders, invoicing, payment tracking, customer portal
  • Operations — varies by industry — manufacturing BOM and production orders, healthcare patient and appointment management, retail point-of-sale and promotion engine
  • HR and payroll — employee records, attendance, leave management, payroll calculation with local tax compliance, payslip generation
  • Analytics and reporting — operational dashboards, management reports, profitability analysis, exportable data for accountants and auditors

How to Evaluate India-Based Custom ERP Developers

The primary risk in hiring any software development partner — India-based or otherwise — is the gap between what is presented during sales and what is delivered during execution. The following evaluation criteria filter for development firms that can actually deliver production-grade ERP systems, not firms that can write impressive proposals.

Ask for live references, not case studies

Request contact details for two or three clients who have received delivered ERP systems in the past 24 months. A firm that cannot or will not provide live references has something to hide about their delivery track record.

Verify the stack depth, not the stack list

Ask specifically: what is your approach to ERP data migration from the client's existing system? How do you handle schema changes in production without downtime? Providers who answer technically have built real systems.

Require milestone-based contracts

Fixed-scope, milestone-based contracts align incentives correctly. Time-and-materials contracts with no defined deliverables shift all project risk to the client. Reputable ERP developers can scope and price milestones.

Confirm team continuity

Insist that the engineers presented during scoping are the engineers who will build the system. High team turnover mid-project is the most common cause of ERP development failure and cost overrun.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does custom ERP software development cost in India?

Custom ERP development from India costs between $15,000 and $120,000 depending on scope. A basic ERP with 4–5 modules costs $15,000 to $30,000. A mid-market ERP with 8–10 modules costs $35,000 to $60,000. A full enterprise ERP with multi-tenant SaaS architecture costs $65,000 to $120,000. These figures are 60–70% lower than equivalent US or UK development costs.

How long does custom ERP development take?

A basic custom ERP takes 3–5 months. A mid-market ERP takes 6–9 months. A full enterprise ERP with multi-tenancy takes 10–18 months. These timelines assume a dedicated team with clearly defined requirements from the start of development.

What technology stack is used for custom ERP development?

Common stacks for custom ERP in India include .NET 8.0 with ASP.NET Core and SQL Server, Java Spring Boot with PostgreSQL or MySQL, and Node.js with React for lighter implementations. The stack choice depends on the client's existing technology environment, team familiarity, and long-term maintenance preferences. T-Mat Global builds ERP systems on .NET 8.0 and Java Spring Boot.

What is the difference between custom ERP and off-the-shelf ERP?

Off-the-shelf ERP products like SAP, Oracle NetSuite, or Microsoft Dynamics are built to serve thousands of business types and require significant configuration, consulting, and process adaptation to deploy. Custom ERP is built specifically for your business model, fits your workflows from day one, requires no licensing fees, and gives you full ownership of the codebase. Custom ERP costs more upfront but is typically cheaper over a five-year horizon for businesses with specific operational requirements.
