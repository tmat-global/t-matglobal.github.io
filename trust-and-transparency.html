Operating Model

How We Operate

T-Mat Global functions as a remote-first, globally distributed technology company headquartered in India, with delivery operations aligned to US, UAE, and UK business hours.

Remote-First Delivery

All engineering, consulting, and support operations are delivered remotely from India. We operate across US Eastern, US Pacific, UAE Gulf Standard, and UK Greenwich time zones with real-time collaboration tools and structured communication protocols.

Agile Engineering Delivery

All project engagements follow a 2-week agile sprint model with daily standups, structured sprint reviews, retrospectives, and documented delivery milestones. Every sprint output is tracked, reported, and signed off by the client.

Cloud-Native Infrastructure

All development environments, deployment pipelines, and client systems are built on modern cloud infrastructure — AWS, Azure, and GCP — with CI/CD automation, containerization, and infrastructure-as-code as standard practice.

AI-Augmented Workflows

Where applicable, T-Mat Global deploys intelligent automation and AI-assisted workflows to improve delivery speed, reduce error rates, and enhance output quality — with full transparency to clients on where AI tooling is applied.

Documented SLA Commitments

Every client engagement is governed by a formally documented Service Level Agreement (SLA) covering response times, escalation paths, delivery timelines, and quality benchmarks — with defined remediation processes for any SLA breach.

Global Delivery Standards

T-Mat Global applies consistent engineering and quality standards across every market we serve — US, UAE, UK, and India — ensuring that clients in any geography receive equivalent levels of delivery rigor, accountability, and support.

Corporate Governance

Governance Framework

T-Mat Global is committed to maintaining the highest standards of corporate governance — ensuring accountability, transparency, and integrity at every level of the organization.

  • 01

    MCA Compliance

    Annual filings, director KYC, and financial disclosures are submitted on time to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013. All corporate records are maintained and available for statutory inspection.

  • 02

    Financial Transparency

    T-Mat Global maintains structured financial records, GST filings, and statutory accounts audited in accordance with Indian accounting standards. Financial integrity is treated as a non-negotiable operational standard.

  • 03

    DPIIT Recognition Compliance

    As a DPIIT Recognized Startup (CERT NO: DIPP248437), T-Mat Global maintains ongoing compliance with the criteria and reporting requirements set by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, Government of India.

  • 04

    Conflict of Interest Policy

    All directors, leadership team members, and senior employees are required to disclose any actual or potential conflicts of interest. Disclosed conflicts are documented, managed, and mitigated in accordance with the company's internal governance policy.

  • 05

    Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption

    T-Mat Global maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward bribery, corruption, and facilitation payments in any form. All employees and contractors are bound by this standard as a condition of engagement.

  • 06

    Whistleblower Protection

    Any employee, contractor, or client who identifies a compliance concern, governance breach, or ethical violation may report it directly to the Founder & CEO. Reports are treated confidentially and investigated promptly.

Data Protection

Data Protection & Privacy

T-Mat Global treats client data, user data, and employee data as a fiduciary responsibility — not merely a compliance obligation.

Data We Collect

T-Mat Global collects only the data necessary to deliver contracted services — including contact information, project requirements, system access credentials (where required for delivery), and communication records. We do not collect or retain data beyond the scope of the engagement.

How We Use Data

Client data is used exclusively for the purpose of delivering agreed services, communicating about the engagement, and fulfilling statutory obligations. Data is never sold, shared with third parties for commercial purposes, or used for marketing without explicit consent.

Data Retention

Project data, communication records, and delivery documentation are retained for a period of 3 years post-engagement closure, unless a different retention period is agreed in writing. After this period, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

NDA & Confidentiality

All client engagements begin with a formally executed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). T-Mat Global treats all client information — including business requirements, technical architecture, commercial terms, and product data — as strictly confidential throughout and after the engagement.

Data Security Measures

Client data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2+. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256. Access to client systems and data is granted on a need-to-know basis, logged, and reviewed regularly. All team members with data access are bound by signed confidentiality obligations.

International Data Transfers

Where client data originates in the US, UAE, or UK and is processed in India, T-Mat Global ensures appropriate contractual safeguards are in place — including standard contractual clauses and data processing agreements — to govern cross-border data flows in compliance with applicable law.

Information Security

Security Standards & Controls

Security is not an afterthought at T-Mat Global — it is embedded into every layer of our engineering, tooling, and operational practices.

Secure Development Lifecycle

All code produced by T-Mat Global undergoes mandatory peer review, static analysis, and security scanning prior to deployment. We integrate security checks into CI/CD pipelines using tools including SonarQube and Trivy.

Access Control

All internal systems, client systems, and development environments are governed by role-based access control (RBAC). Access is granted on a minimum-privilege basis, reviewed quarterly, and revoked immediately upon offboarding.

Vulnerability Management

T-Mat Global conducts regular vulnerability assessments on all systems used in client delivery. Critical and high-severity vulnerabilities are remediated within defined SLA windows. All third-party dependencies are monitored for known CVEs.

Incident Response

T-Mat Global maintains a documented incident response procedure. In the event of a security incident affecting client systems or data, the client is notified within 24 hours of confirmed identification, with a root cause analysis provided within 72 hours.

Backup & Recovery

All client project data and critical operational data is backed up on a daily schedule with off-site redundancy. Recovery point objectives (RPO) and recovery time objectives (RTO) are defined per engagement and documented in the SLA.

Security Awareness

All T-Mat Global team members complete security awareness training at onboarding and on an annual basis. Training covers phishing, social engineering, secure coding practices, data handling obligations, and incident reporting procedures.

Client Standards

Client Engagement Standards

Every T-Mat Global client engagement is governed by a formal contractual and operational framework designed to protect both parties and ensure consistent delivery outcomes.

  • 01

    Formal Engagement Agreement

    Every engagement begins with a signed Statement of Work (SOW) or Master Service Agreement (MSA) that defines scope, deliverables, timelines, commercial terms, escalation procedures, and termination clauses.

  • 02

    Non-Disclosure Agreement

    An NDA is executed prior to the commencement of any substantive discussion about client business, technology, or data. Both parties are bound by mutual confidentiality obligations for the duration of and following the engagement.

  • 03

    Transparent Pricing

    All commercial terms are stated clearly in writing prior to engagement commencement. T-Mat Global does not apply undisclosed fees, scope creep charges, or retroactive billing. Any change to commercial terms requires written mutual agreement.

  • 04

    Delivery Reporting

    Clients receive regular delivery reports — sprint summaries, milestone updates, and issue logs — at agreed intervals. All reports are factual, unembellished, and include both progress and any identified risks or blockers.

  • 05

    Escalation Framework

    Every client has a named escalation path to the Founder & CEO for any delivery, quality, or commercial concern. Escalations are acknowledged within 4 business hours and resolved or formally addressed within 48 hours.

  • 06

    Intellectual Property

    All work product produced for a client under a signed engagement agreement is assigned to the client upon full payment of agreed fees, unless otherwise specified in the contract. T-Mat Global retains no ownership of client-funded IP.

Workforce Practices

Workforce & Employment Standards

T-Mat Global is committed to building a high-performance, ethically managed workforce — with equal standards applied to full-time employees and contracted resources.

Equal Opportunity Employment

T-Mat Global does not discriminate on the basis of gender, religion, caste, ethnicity, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. All hiring, promotion, and compensation decisions are based solely on merit, qualifications, and performance.

Fair Compensation

All employees and contractors are compensated at or above market rates for their role and level of experience. Compensation structures are reviewed annually. T-Mat Global complies fully with applicable minimum wage, PF, ESI, and statutory bonus regulations.

Contractor & 3rd Party Payroll Compliance

All contractors placed under T-Mat Global's 3rd party payroll services are engaged under formally documented contractor agreements. Payroll is processed in compliance with Indian labour law, including TDS deduction, PF contributions, and ESI where applicable.

Working Hours & Wellbeing

T-Mat Global does not mandate excessive working hours. Team members are expected to work within reasonable boundaries, and management actively monitors for signs of unsustainable workload. Mental health and work-life balance are treated as organizational priorities.

Performance Management

Performance reviews are conducted formally on a semi-annual basis with documented feedback, clear objectives, and development plans. No employee is terminated without a documented performance improvement process, unless in cases of gross misconduct.

No Child or Forced Labour

T-Mat Global maintains an absolute prohibition on child labour, forced labour, or any form of exploitative employment practice. This standard applies to direct employees, contractors, and any third-party labour supply engaged in the delivery of our services.

Code of Ethics

Ethical Standards & Code of Conduct

Every person at T-Mat Global — from the Founder to the most recently onboarded contractor — is bound by the same ethical code. These are not aspirational values. They are operational requirements.

Honesty Without Exception

We tell clients the truth — about project status, risks, and limitations — even when it is uncomfortable. We never overstate capabilities, understate timelines, or conceal delivery issues.

Accountability Over Blame

When things go wrong — and they sometimes do — T-Mat Global takes ownership, communicates proactively, and resolves the issue. We do not shift blame, make excuses, or minimize the impact on clients.

No Commercial Pressure on Quality

Commercial considerations — including cost pressures, tight timelines, or client requests — never justify the release of low-quality or insecure code. Quality standards are fixed, not negotiable.

Respect in Every Interaction

All interactions — internal and external — are conducted with professional respect. Harassment, intimidation, or disrespectful conduct of any kind is grounds for immediate termination of employment or contractor engagement.

Transparent Communication

We communicate clearly, in writing, and on time. Meeting commitments, delivering updates without being chased, and keeping clients informed of relevant developments are baseline expectations — not exceptional behavior.

Continuous Improvement Obligation

Every T-Mat Global team member is expected to continuously improve their skills, practices, and processes. Stagnation is treated as a risk — to the individual, to the team, and to the quality of outcomes we deliver.
