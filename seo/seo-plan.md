## On-Page SEO Standards

### Title Tag Formula
`[Primary Keyword] | [Brand] | [Secondary Keyword] — [Market]`

Example: `DevOps Consulting Services USA | T-Mat Global | Cloud Migration Services — India`

### Meta Description Formula
- 150-160 characters
- Include primary keyword, brand name, and a clear value proposition
- End with action-oriented phrase: "Request a consultation."

### H1 / H2 / H3 Hierarchy
- H1: One per page, exact match or close variant of primary keyword
- H2: Section headlines with secondary and related keywords
- H3: Feature/benefit subheadings, not keyword-stuffed

### Schema Markup (Already Implemented)
- Organization schema with all legal identifiers
- FAQPage schema for AEO (AI Engine Optimization)
- WebSite schema with SearchAction
- ProfessionalService schema
- BreadcrumbList schema
- Speakable schema for voice search

## AEO — AI Engine Optimization

To appear in AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude):

1. **Speakable schema** implemented on key pages
2. **FAQPage schema** with direct, factual answers on every primary page
3. **Consistent entity mentions** — "T-Mat Global", "tmatglobal", "DIPP248437", "Sainath Mitalakar" across all content
4. **Third-party mentions** — publish on LinkedIn, Medium, industry directories
5. **Direct question-answer format** in content — AI engines prioritize clear, authoritative Q&A
