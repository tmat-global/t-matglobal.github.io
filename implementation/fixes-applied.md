## Summary: What Changed Conceptually

### Removed From Site
- All team size numbers (6 engineers, 5+ collective years)
- Intern job titles
- 1-year experience credentials
- $200K ARR revenue target (publicly visible)
- "First 3 US clients" goal (exposed zero current clients)
- Self-graded competitive comparison table
- "Startup" and "startup agility" language throughout
- topmate.io scheduling links (signals solo creator, not enterprise)
- WhatsApp as CTA on About Us and Why Us pages
- "Let's Talk", "Start a Conversation", "Let's Build Together" — casual CTAs
- "Built Different. Delivered Different." — consumer brand language
- "Work With Us Your Way" — informal phrasing
- "DPIIT Recognized Startup" repeated in hero sections, cards, footers

### Added or Upgraded
- Operational capability signals replacing small team numbers
- "Government-Recognized Technology Company" as credential framing
- "Purpose-Built for Enterprise Delivery" as positioning headline
- "Delivery Commitments" section — non-comparative, confidence-based
- "Engineering Disciplines" section replacing team roster
- 5-stage delivery model replacing growth roadmap
- "Discuss Your Requirements" and "Submit Your Requirements" as CTAs
- Direct contact form links replacing third-party scheduling tools
- Consistent language across all three pages

## Remaining Gaps (Require Business Action, Not Code)

1. **No client testimonials** — The site needs at least one real, named testimonial with full attribution. This is the single highest-impact trust addition possible.
2. **No quantified delivery outcomes** — Once projects are delivered, publish case studies with specific metrics: deployment time reduction, cost savings, system uptime, throughput improvements.
3. **No US presence signal** — A US phone number or US-based contact email would materially increase trust with American enterprise buyers.
4. **Projects page shows "Pre-Delivery" status** — Once the ERP and LIMS are delivered, update to "Delivered" and add actual outcome statements.
5. **LinkedIn company page visibility** — Enterprise buyers will check the company page to verify team depth. Ensure the page is populated and consistent with site messaging.
