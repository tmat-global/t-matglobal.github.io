About T-Mat Global

T-Mat Global is a DPIIT Recognized Startup under the Startup India Initiative, Government of India (CERT: DIPP248437 · CIN: U62010PN2026PTC252419). Founded by Sainath Shivaji Mitalakar — former DevOps Engineer at T-Mobile USA and AWS Certified Cloud Engineer.

Today, we deliver high-impact DevOps engineering, cloud infrastructure, AI systems, software development, and strategic IT consulting to enterprises across the US, UAE, UK, and beyond. We combine the agility of a startup with the operational maturity of an enterprise partner.

Our engineering teams are built for precision. Our processes are built for accountability. And our culture is built around one unwavering commitment — to deliver outcomes that genuinely move the needle.

Headquartered in India. Engineered for the world. Operating 24/7 across every major time zone.
