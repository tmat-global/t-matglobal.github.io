UAE companies are moving fast on cloud, AI and fintech, and the people who buy from them are asking harder questions. Enterprise customers, regulators and investors increasingly want proof that software is built and shipped securely. If you are searching for DevSecOps services in UAE, finding a provider is easy. The hard part is telling a team that builds security into your pipeline from one that hands you a report and leaves.

This guide explains what DevSecOps services include, what a good provider covers, and the questions to ask before you sign.

## What are DevSecOps services?

DevSecOps services are the consulting, engineering and managed support that build security into every stage of software delivery, from code and CI/CD pipelines to cloud infrastructure and runtime, instead of checking it at the end.

In practice this means automated security testing in your pipeline, secure cloud and Kubernetes configuration, secrets management, vulnerability management and compliance evidence that is produced continuously. Our guide to [manual vs automated DevSecOps](/blog/manual-vs-automated-devsecops) explains why automation is what makes this sustainable.

## Why do UAE businesses need DevSecOps now?

UAE businesses need DevSecOps because customers and regulators are asking for security evidence earlier, and a breach in the build pipeline can expose production systems and customer data.

Three pressures show up again and again:

- **Enterprise and government buyers** send security questionnaires and expect answers about how you build and deploy software.
- **Data protection rules** are tightening. The federal Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) is in force, and free zones such as ADGM and DIFC have their own data protection regimes.
- **Supply chain attacks on CI/CD** are rising. Recent campaigns such as GhostAction showed how a hijacked workflow can steal cloud credentials. Read our guide on [how to secure GitHub Actions workflows](/blog/secure-github-actions-workflows-ghostaction).

## What should a DevSecOps provider in the UAE cover?

A complete provider should cover the pipeline, the cloud, the containers and the compliance evidence, not just one scanning tool.

1. **CI/CD pipeline security:** workflow permissions, secrets handling, pinned dependencies and approval gates.
2. **Application security testing:** SAST, SCA and DAST wired into the pipeline, mapped to OWASP guidance.
3. **Cloud security:** AWS, Azure or Google Cloud configuration reviewed against CIS Benchmarks, with least-privilege IAM.
4. **Container and Kubernetes security:** image scanning, admission policies and runtime controls.
5. **Infrastructure as code security:** Terraform and CloudFormation checks before anything is deployed.
6. **Vulnerability management:** prioritised findings, owners and fix timelines, so issues close instead of piling up. See our guide to [reducing mean time to remediation](/blog/reduce-mean-time-to-remediation-guide).
7. **Compliance automation:** evidence for SOC 2, ISO 27001 and PCI DSS collected continuously, plus SBOM generation. Our post on [continuous compliance and SBOM](/blog/continuous-compliance-sbom) covers this.
8. **Incident readiness:** clear escalation paths and visibility when something goes wrong.

## How do you evaluate a DevSecOps provider?

Evaluate a provider on proof of hands-on pipeline work, clarity about deliverables and how they handle your access and data, not on the length of their service list.

Ask these questions:

- **What will be running in our pipeline after you finish?** Good answers name specific automated checks, not just a written report.
- **Who does the work, and what is their production experience?** Look for engineers with real enterprise delivery behind them and recognised certifications such as AWS Certified DevOps Engineer Professional.
- **How do you map your work to our compliance needs?** A provider should talk about the frameworks that apply to you, such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS and PDPL, and be clear that legal interpretation belongs with your counsel.
- **How do you handle access to our cloud accounts and code?** Expect least-privilege access, time-limited credentials, NDAs and audit trails.
- **Where will our data and workloads reside?** Ask about UAE data residency options with your cloud provider if that matters for your sector.
- **What happens after the project?** Check whether you own all pipelines, policies and documentation, and whether ongoing support is optional.
- **How will we communicate?** Regular check-ins, a named lead and a clear escalation path.

## Should you hire in-house, use a consultancy or choose managed DevSecOps?

The right model depends on your team size and how fast you need results.

- **In-house:** best when you already have several engineers and a steady security workload. It is the slowest and most expensive to build.
- **Consultancy project:** best for a defined goal, such as a pipeline hardening sprint, a cloud security review or audit preparation.
- **Managed DevSecOps:** best for startups and lean teams that need continuous coverage without hiring a full security team. We explain the model in [what a managed DevSecOps platform is](/blog/what-is-managed-devsecops-platform).

Many teams start with an assessment, then move to a project or a managed engagement once priorities are clear.

## Can an offshore DevSecOps team work well for UAE companies?

Yes, when the engagement is structured properly. India and the UAE are only about 1.5 hours apart, so working hours overlap almost fully, and calls and incident response fit normal business days.

What matters more than location is control. Insist on least-privilege access, separate accounts or roles for the provider, written confidentiality terms, and logs of what was changed. Providers that work this way are easy to audit, wherever they sit.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are DevSecOps services?

DevSecOps services embed security into software delivery through automated pipeline checks, secure cloud and container configuration, vulnerability management and continuous compliance evidence.

### Why do UAE companies need DevSecOps?

Enterprise customers, regulators and investors increasingly ask how software is built and secured, and pipeline attacks can expose cloud credentials and customer data. DevSecOps reduces that risk and speeds up security reviews.

### How much do DevSecOps services cost in the UAE?

Cost depends on scope, team size and engagement model. Assessments, fixed-scope projects and monthly managed plans are priced differently, so ask each provider for a written scope and deliverables before comparing quotes.

### Is DevSecOps only for large enterprises?

No. Startups often benefit most, because early pipeline and cloud decisions are cheaper to get right than to fix later, and enterprise buyers ask smaller companies for security evidence too.

### Does the UAE PDPL apply to my business?

The federal PDPL applies across the UAE, except within the DIFC and ADGM financial free zones, which have their own data protection regimes. Confirm how it applies to you with UAE legal counsel, as implementation rules are still developing.

### What certifications should a DevSecOps provider have?

Look for relevant cloud certifications such as AWS Certified DevOps Engineer Professional, plus demonstrable experience with SOC 2, ISO 27001 and PCI DSS programmes. Certifications show knowledge, but ask for examples of delivered pipeline work too.

### Can a provider outside the UAE support my team?

Yes, if access is controlled with least privilege, confidentiality terms and audit logs. Time zone overlap between India and the UAE is strong, which makes day-to-day collaboration practical.

### How long does it take to improve pipeline security?

A focused assessment can take days, and core fixes such as permissions, secrets and automated scanning can often land within a few weeks. Full compliance programmes take longer and depend on your starting point.
